Privacy policy

Last updated 10/10/2026.

Who uses it. Only Benjamin Bruce, for his own accounts.

Removing access. Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Facebook's business integrations settings. Stored data can be deleted from his computer at any time.
